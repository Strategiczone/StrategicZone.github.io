About Strategic Zone Intelligence

Intelligence built around precision.

We are a global market research and fieldwork partner helping enterprises transform research objectives into reliable, validated and decision-ready data.

01

Who We Are

Strategic Zone Intelligence is a market research and global fieldwork organization built around more than a decade of experience in B2B and B2C research. Our operating model combines respondent recruitment, panel management, survey programming support, CATI and CAWI execution, multilingual fieldwork and research quality assurance.

Our regional capabilities allow research teams to access audiences across APAC, AMER, EMEA and ISME while maintaining consistent fieldwork standards and transparent quality processes.

02

What We Do

We provide end-to-end quantitative and qualitative fieldwork execution. From sample design and respondent recruitment to survey completion, validation and delivery, our teams support complex research programs with operational discipline.

Our capabilities include B2B decision-maker recruitment, B2C audience targeting, CATI, CAWI, IDIs, focus groups and specialist audience research.

03

Why Join Us

For panelists, we provide access to relevant research opportunities while respecting privacy, eligibility and participation requirements. For global enterprise clients, we offer a structured route to qualified audiences and fieldwork execution.

Our focus is simple: dependable respondents, clean data, responsive fieldwork and clear communication throughout the project lifecycle.

04

Our Quality Control Framework

Quality is integrated into our workflow rather than treated as a final-stage check. Our framework can incorporate digital fingerprinting, duplicate detection, anti-bot validation, response-pattern monitoring, geo and device signals, respondent verification and project-level quality audits.

We continuously monitor fieldwork performance to identify anomalies early and maintain confidence in delivered research data.
